# MVP automatic card generation
Keep existing nickname, number, position, hand and declarations; accept 1–3 photos, detect people rather than requiring faces. Main photo determines pose; references preserve identity. Three fixed series: classic photographic, AURA artistic subject and imaginary defocused background, ANIMATION coherent Rick and Morty-style American cartoon subject and background. Fixed Blueprint chrome logo and player name footer are rendered deterministically outside AI artwork. Silver, gold concentric rings and prism facets respond dynamically to pointer/tilt. Export captures material, not just raw art.
OpenAI is the explicitly selected provider. Secrets server-only. Missing key is clearly reported, never silently replaced with mock output. No automatic paid retries. Authenticate generation with signed existing session, validate image limits/input and ownership, deduplicate concurrent requests, bound resource use. Existing quota is charged once after successful rendering. All uploaded photos processed under updated explicit consent.
